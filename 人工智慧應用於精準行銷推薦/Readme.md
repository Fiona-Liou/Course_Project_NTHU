# 1. Self-Supervised Learning in Recommender Systems

This project explores the cutting-edge landscape of Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) and its potential to revolutionize the retail industry by solving the persistent challenge of "information overload".

## Highlights
- Systematic Taxonomy: Categorized SSL methodologies from over 170+ academic papers into three primary frameworks: Contrastive, Reconstructive, and Adversarial learning.
- Algorithmic Deep-Dive:
  - Analyzed how Generative Models (VAE, MAE, and Denoising Diffusion) capture underlying data patterns to enhance recommendation performance.
  - Explored the use of InfoNCE and Mutual Information to maximize agreement between augmented data views.
- Retail Implementation Proposal: Proposed the CroRec (Cross-domain Recommendation) framework for UNIQLO to bridge the gap between LINE behavioral data and App purchase history, effectively addressing the cold-start problem.

## Keywords
- Recommender Systems 
- Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) 
- Generative AI Data Sparsity 
- Cross-domain Recommendation




# 2. UNIQLO Precision Marketing Strategy: Data-Driven Business Analysis

This project analyzes how UNIQLO utilizes an OMO (Online-Merge-Offline) ecosystem and advanced data analytics to drive "LifeWear" global growth.

## Strategic Insights
- OMO & Supply Chain Optimization: Analyzed the impact of RFID smart replenishment systems, which reduced inventory counting time by 80%.
- Precision Marketing via LINE CRM:
  - Evaluated customer segmentation strategies (New Comers, Inactive, Commuters) using behavioral labels for targeted remarketing.
  - Case Study: Digital "Scratch Card" campaigns resulted in participants consuming 1.25x more than average users.
- AI-Driven Engagement: Analyzed the role of the U-Butler (Chatbot), which collected over 1.8M data points and contributed to a 10% increase in sales.

## Outcomes
- Demonstrated how Data-driven Insights transform raw behavioral data into actionable marketing strategies.
- Showcased a holistic understanding of how MarTech (Marketing Technology) enhances customer loyalty and conversion rates.

## Keywords
- Precision Marketing 
- OMO Strategy 
- CRM Analytics 
- Consumer Segmentation 
- Retail Tech
