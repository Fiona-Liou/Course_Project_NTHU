## Dataset intro:

The dataset is derived from the USDA Food Environment Atlas, which provides county-level indicators on food access, food stores, restaurants, food assistance programs, local food systems, health outcomes, and socioeconomic variables across the United States. It aims to capture how local food environments affect people’s health, nutrition, and community development. 
In this project, we mainly focus on variables related to the local food environment (e.g. farmers’ market density, direct farm sales, and CSA farms) and health status (such as adult obesity and diabetes rates). Socioeconomic factors are also included as control variables.

## Data set: Food Environment Atlas from Economic Research Service
